SCOPE AND EVIDENCE
The canonical catalog is lib/plants/catalog.ts. The registry and commercial report
are derived exports, not parallel runtime matching systems. All 91 existing entries,
their indexed URLs, SEO fields, gallery and rights/provenance records are preserved.
Six additions bring the candidate to 97 species/horticultural entities. Genus and
family landing pages are listed separately and are not counted as species.

Every entity received structural identity/content/SEO/media/link triage. The labels
CONTENT_COMPLETE and CONTENT_GOOD measure documented field coverage, not a claim
that every historical scientific sentence was freshly reviewed by a botanist.
Sources already attached to historical entries remain evidence, with limitations
listed per record. Targeted factual corrections and six additions were researched.
Two thin ambiguous Anthurium records cannot safely be enriched as certain crosses.
Three existing identities require evidence: those two Anthurium records and the
historical Equisetum japonicum label. No uncertain identity was silently invented.

SAFE CORRECTIONS
28 prior entries receive targeted care, alias, parent-link, safety or field updates.
Eight epithet-only species fields become binomials without changing indexed URLs.
Unknown toxicity no longer means a false non-toxic filter; Aloe vera, Plumeria rubra
and Yucca rostrata warnings use specific institutional sources. Equisetum is not
flowering. Propagation advice now distinguishes nodes, offsets, rhizomes and seeds.
Substring-based comparison resolution was replaced by exact, collision-safe names.
Uncertain comparisons lead to the appropriate genus with an honest link label.
Philodendron New Red is not asserted to be Royal Queen without identity evidence.
Monstera Squeleto is a search alias, not a new species.

ROUTES AND DUPLICATES
No duplicate canonical names or slugs were found; no entity was deleted or merged.
Nine declared aliases had no compiled redirect routes. Three representative actual
PUBLIC requests (Jacklyn, amazonica, Variegata) returned 404 before this patch.
All nine now return 301 in compiled tests. Amazonica points to the genus, not Polly;
Variegata points to the general deliciosa Variegata page, not automatically Albo.
Every canonical entity has a legitimate HTML genus referrer. All encyclopedia HTML
internal destination/fragment checks pass. Existing canonical URLs remain unchanged.

COMMERCIAL LIMIT
The actual public Shop /api/catalogue request returned 404 while the homepage showed
the coming-soon publication state. Its existing source contract confirms that this
endpoint is unavailable in that state. No private catalog access was fabricated.
The denominator is therefore ONLY the 51 supplied reference names: 24 exact,
22 horticultural-form, one species-level, two ambiguous and two insufficient.
47/51 = 92.16% safe documented coverage; it is NOT a certified live-Shop KPI.
No high-confidence missing page remains within this supplied reference set.
Beaucarnea does not imply recurvata; Nepenthes mix does not imply a species.
Maranta Lemon needs the fuller label; Strelitzia augusta needs nomenclatural versus
commercial usage clarification. All evidence requests are batched in one report.
